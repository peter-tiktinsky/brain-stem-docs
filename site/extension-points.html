System personalization · where your additions go

Extension Points

Everything you add to Brain Stem lives in a place an upgrade leaves alone. This page names that place, shows how to audit it, and explains the three mechanisms that keep it safe.

How to build what goes here is on Build your own capabilities.

Your capability root

A capability root is a skill folder of your own, outside the shipped list of skills, holding three things that move together:

  • capability-registry.json, with a schema_version and a capabilities map;
  • SKILL.md, with one ## Capability: <name> heading for each registered capability;
  • capabilities/<name>.sh, one executable script for each capability.

Build it with the scaffold rather than by hand: scaffold.sh skill <name> creates the folder, and scaffold.sh capability <name> <cap> adds a capability to it.

Audit it with --root

The registry parity check audits the shipped librarian by default. Point it at your own root instead: 

bash ~/.claude/skills/librarian/capabilities/capability-registry-parity.sh --root <your-skill-folder>
  • A root written by the scaffold reports no findings. Findings go to standard output, or to the file named by FINDINGS_OUTPUT.
  • --root takes an existing directory. A missing directory or no value stops with exit 2.
  • Under --root the checks that look at the shipped home skip, because the folders beside your root belong to the shipped home and not to you.
  • Without --root the run is the shipped-librarian audit, and your root never appears in it.

Lint your frontmatter with skill-parity

bash ~/.claude/skills/librarian/capabilities/skill-parity.sh --scope <your-skill-folder>
FindingWhat it means
skill-parity-unknown-frontmatter-keyA top-level key Claude Code does not recognize. The platform ignores it without an error, so a mistyped descripton: does nothing and says nothing. Keep your own data under the metadata map, whose nested keys are not read
skill-parity-listing-lengthThe skill’s description plus when_to_use is longer than the skill listing keeps (1,536 characters combined), so the end of it would be cut

What survives an upgrade, and why

An upgrade replaces what Brain Stem ships and leaves what is yours. Three mechanisms hold that line.

MechanismWhy your additions are safe
The skill listThe installer copies skills from a named list, one folder at a time. A skill folder outside that list is never copied over. Keep yours under its own name, never under the name of a shipped skill
The settings.json mergeThe installer compares every path in your settings.json before and after the merge and stops with an error if any would vanish, so your hooks, permissions and other keys stay
The hook reconcilerIt appends a missing shipped hook to the right matcher and never removes or reorders a hook of yours. A shipped hook you turned off with field-set.sh settings hook off is left out, as Configuration describes
A shipped skill folder is not yours

A shipped skill folder belongs to the installer, and edits inside it are not protected. Put additions in your own folder.
